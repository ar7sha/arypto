# Security Policy

This is a fun project. No audit. No promises. No bug bounty. 💸

## Found a bug?

Don't open a public issue. Go to the **Security** tab → **"Report a vulnerability"**.

Tell me what you found, how to break it, and how bad it is. I'll answer as fast as a hobby project allows. 🐢

**Your account stays anonymous unless you want credit.** I won't publicly mention your username unless you say it's okay.

## Scope

Not audited. Not production-ready. If you break Rust stdlib or the crypto crates, tell their maintainers — not me. 🙏

## What I won't do

- Pay a bug bounty 💸 (I'm broke)
- Sue you for reporting in good faith 🤝

Thanks! 🚀
